How to read the tank

  • Height is altitude. The surface is 45,000 ft and the sand is the ground.
  • Left and right is west and east of you. The glowing beacon on the sand is where you are.
  • Size and haze show north and south. Fish to the north are smaller and fainter.
  • Color is the airline, so flights from the same airline swim in matching schools.
  • Bubbles trail aircraft that are climbing.
  • Sonar in the corner is the top-down view. Tap it to enlarge it.

Field guide

    Live positions from ADSB.lol, airplanes.live or OpenSky Network, crowd-sourced from ADS-B receivers. Positions can lag by a few seconds and some aircraft are not shown. Not for navigation.
